# Shared Household Chores — Small MVP

## Core Features
1. **Task management** — create, edit, and schedule one-time or recurring chores.
2. **Assignment** — assign chores to household members or let members claim available tasks.
3. **Shared dashboard** — view chores, assignees, due dates, and statuses.
4. **Simple history** — review completed chores and who completed them.

## Purpose
A lightweight web app that helps any household organize chores and see who is responsible for each task.

## Users and Access
- **Organizer:** creates the household, invites members, and manages chores.
- **Member:** views household chores, claims tasks where available, and marks assigned chores complete.
- Users can belong to more than one household and switch between them.
- Household data is visible only to that household's members.

## Basic Requirements
- Support one-time and recurring chores.
- Each chore has a title, optional description, due date, assignee(s), and status.
- Statuses: **To do**, **In progress**, **Completed**, and **Overdue**.
- Allow a chore to be assigned to one or more members.
- Members can mark their chores complete.
- Keep a simple completion history visible to household members.
- Store accounts, household membership, chores, and history persistently.

## Out of Scope
- Email or push notifications.
- Household chat and task comments.
- Points, badges, and gamification.
- Advanced analytics, shopping lists, and native mobile apps.

## MVP Success Criteria
The MVP is successful when household members can join a household, manage and assign chores, track task statuses on a shared dashboard, and review completed chores.
